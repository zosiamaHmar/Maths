# Question 24 — Find the 25th term

##  The sum of the first 14 terms of an AP is 1505 and its first term is 10. Find its 25th term.

- **Write down what we know**
  - `a = 10,   n = 14,   S₁₄ = 1505`
- **Use the sum formula**
  - `1505 = {14|2} [2(10) + (14 − 1)d]`
  - `1505 = 7 [20 + 13d]`
- **Divide both sides by 7**
  - `{1505|7} = 20 + 13d`
  - `215 = 20 + 13d`
- **Subtract 20 from both sides**
  - `195 = 13d`
- **Divide by 13**
  - `d = {195|13} = 15`
- **Now find the 25th term**
  - `a₂₅ = a + (25 − 1)d`
  - `= 10 + 24 × 15`
  - `= 10 + 360`
  - `a₂₅ = 370`

**Answer:** `a₂₅ = 370`

- *Check — Quick check: S₁₄ with d = 15*: `a₁₄ = 10 + 13 × 15 = 205,   S₁₄ = 7 (10 + 205) = 7 × 215 = 1505 ✓`
