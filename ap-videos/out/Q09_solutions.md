# Question 9 — Finding a term from Sₙ

##  The sum of n terms of an AP is  {5n²|2} + {3n|2}.  Find its 20th term.

- **We use: the 20th term is S₂₀ minus S₁₉**
  - `a₂₀ = S₂₀ − S₁₉`
- **First find S₂₀. Put n = 20**
  - `S₂₀ = {5(20)²|2} + {3(20)|2}`
  - `= {5(400)|2} + {60|2}`
  - `= {2000|2} + 30`
  - `= 1000 + 30 = 1030`
- **Now find S₁₉. Put n = 19**
  - `S₁₉ = {5(19)²|2} + {3(19)|2}`
  - `= {5(361)|2} + {57|2}`
  - `= {1805|2} + {57|2}`
  - `= {1862|2} = 931`
- **Subtract**
  - `a₂₀ = 1030 − 931`
  - `a₂₀ = 99`

**Answer:** `a₂₀ = 99`

- *Check — Quick check using the general formula aₙ = 5n − 1*: `a₂₀ = 5(20) − 1 = 99`
