# Question 6 — Finding a and d from two terms

## (i) Find the sum of the first 21 terms of the AP whose 2nd term is 8 and 4th term is 14.

- **Write the 2nd and 4th terms using aₙ = a + (n − 1)d**
  - `a₂ = a + d = 8      ...  (1)`
  - `a₄ = a + 3d = 14      ...  (2)`
- **Subtract equation (1) from equation (2). The a cancels**
  - `(a + 3d) − (a + d) = 14 − 8`
  - `2d = 6`
- **Divide by 2**
  - `d = 3`
- **Put d = 3 into equation (1)**
  - `a + 3 = 8`
  - `a = 8 − 3 = 5`
- **Now the sum of 21 terms**
  - `S₂₁ = {21|2} [2(5) + (21 − 1)(3)]`
- **Work inside the bracket**
  - `= {21|2} [10 + 60]`
  - `= {21|2} × 70`
- **70 divided by 2 is 35, then multiply**
  - `= 21 × 35`
  - `= 735`

**Answer:** `S₂₁ = 735`

- *Check — Quick check: the 21st term is 5 + 20 × 3 = 65*: `S₂₁ = {21|2} (5 + 65) = {21|2} × 70 = 735`

## (ii) Find the sum of the first 51 terms of the AP whose 2nd term is 2 and 4th term is 8.

- **Write the 2nd and 4th terms**
  - `a + d = 2      ...  (1)`
  - `a + 3d = 8      ...  (2)`
- **Subtract equation (1) from equation (2)**
  - `2d = 6`
  - `d = 3`
- **Put d = 3 into equation (1)**
  - `a + 3 = 2`
  - `a = 2 − 3 = −1`
- **Now the sum of 51 terms**
  - `S₅₁ = {51|2} [2(−1) + (51 − 1)(3)]`
- **Work inside the bracket**
  - `= {51|2} [−2 + 150]`
  - `= {51|2} × 148`
- **148 divided by 2 is 74, then multiply 51 by 74**
  - `= 51 × 74`
  - `= 51 × 70 + 51 × 4`
  - `= 3570 + 204`
  - `= 3774`

**Answer:** `S₅₁ = 3774`

- *Check — Quick check: the 51st term is −1 + 50 × 3 = 149*: `S₅₁ = {51|2} (−1 + 149) = {51|2} × 148 = 3774`

## (iii) If the 5th and 12th terms of an AP are −4 and −18, find the sum of the first 20 terms.

- **Write the 5th and 12th terms**
  - `a₅ = a + 4d = −4      ...  (1)`
  - `a₁₂ = a + 11d = −18      ...  (2)`
- **Subtract equation (1) from equation (2)**
  - `(a + 11d) − (a + 4d) = −18 − (−4)`
  - `7d = −18 + 4`
  - `7d = −14`
- **Divide by 7**
  - `d = −2`
- **Put d = −2 into equation (1)**
  - `a + 4(−2) = −4`
  - `a − 8 = −4`
  - `a = −4 + 8 = 4`
- **Now the sum of 20 terms**
  - `S₂₀ = {20|2} [2(4) + (20 − 1)(−2)]`
- **Work inside the bracket**
  - `= 10 [8 + 19(−2)]`
  - `= 10 [8 − 38]`
  - `= 10 × (−30)`
  - `= −300`

**Answer:** `S₂₀ = −300`

- *Check — Quick check: the 20th term is 4 + 19(−2) = −34*: `S₂₀ = 10 (4 − 34) = 10 × (−30) = −300`
