# Question 10 — Which term? and the sum

##  Which term of the AP 5, 9, 13, 17, … is 81? Also find the sum 5 + 9 + 13 + 17 + … + 81.

- **Find a and d**
  - `a = 5,   d = 9 − 5 = 4`
- **Set the nth term equal to 81**
  - `aₙ = a + (n − 1)d`
  - `81 = 5 + (n − 1)(4)`
- **Subtract 5 from both sides**
  - `81 − 5 = 4(n − 1)`
  - `76 = 4(n − 1)`
- **Divide by 4, then add 1**
  - `n − 1 = {76|4} = 19`
  - `n = 20`
- **So 81 is the 20th term. Now the sum of all 20 terms**
  - `S₂₀ = {20|2} (5 + 81)`
- **Add inside the bracket, then multiply**
  - `= 10 × 86`
  - `= 860`

**Answer:** `81 is the 20th term,   Sum = 860`

- *Check — Quick check with the other formula*: `S₂₀ = 10 [10 + 19 × 4] = 10 × 86 = 860`
