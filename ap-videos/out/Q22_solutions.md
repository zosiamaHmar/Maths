# Question 22 — Common difference from a, l and the sum

##  In an AP the first term is 2, the last term is 29 and the sum of the terms is 155. Find the common difference.

- **Write down what we know**
  - `a = 2,   l = 29,   Sₙ = 155`
- **Use the second sum formula to find n**
  - `Sₙ = {n|2} (a + l)`
  - `155 = {n|2} (2 + 29)`
  - `155 = {n|2} × 31`
- **Multiply both sides by 2**
  - `310 = 31n`
- **Divide by 31**
  - `n = {310|31} = 10`
- **Now use the nth term formula with l = 29**
  - `l = a + (n − 1)d`
  - `29 = 2 + (10 − 1)d`
  - `29 = 2 + 9d`
- **Subtract 2 from both sides**
  - `27 = 9d`
- **Divide by 9**
  - `d = 3`

**Answer:** `d = 3`

- *Check — Quick check: the terms are 2, 5, 8, 11, 14, 17, 20, 23, 26, 29*: `Sum = 5 × (2 + 29) = 5 × 31 = 155 ✓`
