# Question 17 — Two clues about an AP

##  The sum of the first six terms of an AP is 42. The ratio of its 10th term to its 30th term is 1 : 3. Calculate the first and the thirteenth term.

- **Clue 1: the sum of 6 terms is 42**
  - `S₆ = {6|2} [2a + 5d] = 42`
  - `3 (2a + 5d) = 42`
- **Divide by 3**
  - `2a + 5d = 14      ...  (1)`
- **Clue 2: the ratio of the 10th term to the 30th is 1 to 3**
  - `{a₁₀|a₃₀} = {1|3}`
  - `{a + 9d|a + 29d} = {1|3}`
- **Cross multiply**
  - `3 (a + 9d) = 1 (a + 29d)`
  - `3a + 27d = a + 29d`
- **Collect a on one side and d on the other**
  - `3a − a = 29d − 27d`
  - `2a = 2d`
  - `a = d      ...  (2)`
- **Put a = d into equation (1)**
  - `2d + 5d = 14`
  - `7d = 14`
  - `d = 2`
- **Then a equals d**
  - `a = 2`
- **Now the 13th term**
  - `a₁₃ = a + 12d = 2 + 12(2)`
  - `= 2 + 24`
  - `a₁₃ = 26`

**Answer:** `First term = 2,   13th term = 26`

- *Check — Quick check: the AP is 2, 4, 6, 8, …*: `S₆ = 2 + 4 + 6 + 8 + 10 + 12 = 42 ✓`; `a₁₀ : a₃₀ = 20 : 60 = 1 : 3 ✓`
