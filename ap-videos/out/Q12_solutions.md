# Question 12 — nth term, first term and d from Sₙ

##  If the sum of the first n terms of an AP is Sₙ = 3n² − n, find its (i) nth term, (ii) first term and (iii) common difference.

- **(i) Use aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁. First write Sₙ₋₁**
  - `Sₙ₋₁ = 3(n − 1)² − (n − 1)`
- **Expand (n − 1) squared**
  - `= 3(n² − 2n + 1) − n + 1`
  - `= 3n² − 6n + 3 − n + 1`
  - `= 3n² − 7n + 4`
- **Subtract**
  - `aₙ = (3n² − n) − (3n² − 7n + 4)`
  - `= 3n² − n − 3n² + 7n − 4`
- **3n² cancels. Combine the n terms**
  - `aₙ = 6n − 4`
- **(ii) First term: put n = 1**
  - `a₁ = 6(1) − 4`
  - `a₁ = 2`
- **(iii) Common difference: find a₂ and subtract**
  - `a₂ = 6(2) − 4 = 8`
  - `d = a₂ − a₁ = 8 − 2`
  - `d = 6`

**Answer:** `aₙ = 6n − 4,   a = 2,   d = 6`

- *Check — Quick check: S₁ = 3 − 1 = 2, and S₂ = 12 − 2 = 10 = 2 + 8*: `S₂ = 10 ✓`
