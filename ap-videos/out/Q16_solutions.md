# Question 16 — nth term from the sum

##  If Sₙ, the sum of the first n terms of an AP, is Sₙ = 5n² + 3n, find its nth term.

- **We use: aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁**
  - `aₙ = Sₙ − Sₙ₋₁`
- **Write Sₙ₋₁ by replacing n with n − 1**
  - `Sₙ₋₁ = 5(n − 1)² + 3(n − 1)`
- **Expand (n − 1) squared**
  - `= 5(n² − 2n + 1) + 3n − 3`
  - `= 5n² − 10n + 5 + 3n − 3`
  - `= 5n² − 7n + 2`
- **Subtract. Watch the signs**
  - `aₙ = (5n² + 3n) − (5n² − 7n + 2)`
  - `= 5n² + 3n − 5n² + 7n − 2`
- **5n² cancels. Combine the n terms**
  - `aₙ = 10n − 2`

**Answer:** `aₙ = 10n − 2`

- *Check — Quick check: a₁ = S₁*: `a₁ = 10 − 2 = 8,   S₁ = 5 + 3 = 8 ✓`
