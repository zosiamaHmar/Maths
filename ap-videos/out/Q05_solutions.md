# Question 5 — Sum from the nth term

## (i) Find the sum of the first 25 terms of an AP whose nth term is aₙ = 7 − 3n.

- **Put n = 1 to get the first term**
  - `a₁ = 7 − 3(1) = 7 − 3 = 4`
- **Put n = 2 to get the second term**
  - `a₂ = 7 − 3(2) = 7 − 6 = 1`
- **The common difference is second minus first**
  - `d = 1 − 4 = −3`
- **Put n = 25 to get the 25th term**
  - `a₂₅ = 7 − 3(25) = 7 − 75 = −68`
- **Use the second sum formula**
  - `Sₙ = {n|2} (a + l)`
  - `S₂₅ = {25|2} (4 + (−68))`
- **Add inside the bracket, then multiply**
  - `= {25|2} × (−64)`
  - `= 25 × (−32)`
  - `= −800`

**Answer:** `S₂₅ = −800`

- *Check — Quick check with the other formula*: `S₂₅ = {25|2} [8 + 24(−3)] = {25|2} × (−64) = −800`

## (ii) Find the sum of the first 20 terms of an AP whose nth term is aₙ = 2 − 3n.

- **Put n = 1 to get the first term**
  - `a₁ = 2 − 3(1) = −1`
- **Put n = 2 to get the second term**
  - `a₂ = 2 − 3(2) = −4`
- **The common difference**
  - `d = −4 − (−1) = −3`
- **Put n = 20 to get the 20th term**
  - `a₂₀ = 2 − 3(20) = 2 − 60 = −58`
- **Use the second sum formula**
  - `S₂₀ = {20|2} (−1 + (−58))`
- **Add inside the bracket, then multiply**
  - `= 10 × (−59)`
  - `= −590`

**Answer:** `S₂₀ = −590`

- *Check — Quick check with the other formula*: `S₂₀ = 10 [−2 + 19(−3)] = 10 × (−59) = −590`
