# Question 23 — Find the AP

##  In an AP the sum of the first ten terms is −150 and the sum of its next ten terms is −550. Find the AP.

- **The first ten terms add up to −150**
  - `S₁₀ = −150`
- **The first twenty terms are the first ten plus the next ten**
  - `S₂₀ = −150 + (−550) = −700`
- **Use the sum formula for 10 terms**
  - `{10|2} [2a + 9d] = −150`
  - `5 (2a + 9d) = −150`
- **Divide by 5**
  - `2a + 9d = −30      ...  (1)`
- **Use the sum formula for 20 terms**
  - `{20|2} [2a + 19d] = −700`
  - `10 (2a + 19d) = −700`
- **Divide by 10**
  - `2a + 19d = −70      ...  (2)`
- **Subtract equation (1) from equation (2)**
  - `(2a + 19d) − (2a + 9d) = −70 − (−30)`
  - `10d = −70 + 30`
  - `10d = −40`
  - `d = −4`
- **Put d = −4 into equation (1)**
  - `2a + 9(−4) = −30`
  - `2a − 36 = −30`
  - `2a = 6`
  - `a = 3`
- **Build the AP: add d = −4 each time**
  - `3, −1, −5, −9, −13, …`

**Answer:** `The AP is 3, −1, −5, −9, …`

- *Check — Quick check: the first ten terms go 3 down to −33*: `S₁₀ = 5 (3 − 33) = 5 × (−30) = −150 ✓`
